# A simple assessment

Review the actual project sources, SPEC.md, recreation guide, issue/PR and short LEARNING.md record together. Assess evidence and explanations, not polished AI writing, prompt counts or unquestioning agreement. Hints are welcome; disclose them.

| Criterion | Clear evidence | Some evidence | Not yet evidenced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explain the existing system | The student connects components and delivery steps to their purpose and explains an actual observation. | The main path is identified, with specific unexplained connections. | Only AI prose or approval is available. |
| Document a usable recreation | Ordered, location-specific steps link actual sources/private-input needs to expected results; attempted scope and remaining gaps are explicit. | A useful guide exists, but missing steps or evidence limit recreation. | Sources, required steps or enough evidence to inspect the claim are unavailable. |
| Check and improve | The student interprets one actual check, reports their own observation, and explains a correction, limit or keep/revise decision. | Checks or observations exist, but their meaning or resulting decision needs clarification. | Only passing-status claims or AI observations are available. |

These descriptions support human judgment; the AI does not assign scores or certify understanding. Honest gaps do not count as verified functionality. A missing statement is not proof the student cannot explain it: ask one concrete follow-up about their actual system when necessary.

LEARNING.md is an AI-drafted record of available task contributions, not a transcript, automatic recording system or proof of authorship. Distinguish learner words, assisted explanations and tool results. Its accuracy approval does not supply missing understanding. Let students correct/remove excerpts before sharing; public Git history can retain earlier versions.
